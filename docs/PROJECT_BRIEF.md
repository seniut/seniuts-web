# seniuts.com — Project Brief

## Company

**seniuts.com** is a data consulting company based in Poland and working globally.

The company helps clients design, launch, and transfer modern data platforms.

## Founder background

The founder is an experienced Data Engineer / Data Architect with around 8 years of experience in:

- Python
- Scala
- Spark
- SQL
- AWS
- Azure
- Kafka
- Airflow
- Delta Lake / Lakehouse architecture
- Data platform design
- Data pipelines
- Cloud-native data engineering

## Business model

seniuts.com should not be positioned as a long-term outsourcing vendor.

The preferred model is:

1. Understand the client's business and data situation.
2. Design a practical target architecture.
3. Build a PoC or MVP.
4. Launch the platform foundation.
5. Enable or assemble the client's team.
6. Transfer ownership.
7. Optionally provide short-term stabilization support.

## Core positioning

Main positioning statement:

> We design and launch production-ready data platforms, then transfer ownership to your team.

Alternative positioning statements:

> Data platform consulting for companies that need architecture, delivery, and clean handover.

> From data chaos to production-ready foundations — with a clear transfer plan.

> Architecture, PoC, launch, and handover for modern data platforms.

## Main offer

The main entry offer is:

> Architecture & Strategy Audit

This should be the primary CTA across the website.

## Service packages

### Architecture & Strategy Audit

For companies that need clarity before investing in a data platform.

Outcome:
A clear architecture, roadmap, risks, and next steps.

### PoC / MVP Delivery

For companies that need to prove the architecture quickly.

Outcome:
A working foundation with selected sources, orchestration, data quality, and documentation.

### Launch & Transfer

For companies that need a production-ready foundation and ownership transfer.

Outcome:
A working platform, engineering standards, runbooks, onboarding, and optional stabilization support.
