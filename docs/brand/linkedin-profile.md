## Current About section

I help companies design, build, and improve production-ready data platforms with a focus on architecture, delivery, scalability, and long-term ownership.

I work at the intersection of hands-on data engineering, technical leadership, and data platform architecture. My experience covers cloud-native data platforms, Lakehouse architecture, batch and streaming pipelines, orchestration, infrastructure automation, and team enablement.

What I usually help with:

• Data platform architecture and roadmap  
• Lakehouse architecture with Delta Lake / Apache Iceberg concepts  
• Spark, PySpark, Python, Scala, and Polars pipelines  
• Airflow, Dagster, and Azure Data Factory orchestration  
• Kafka, kSQL, and event-driven data processing  
• AWS, Azure, Databricks, Synapse, Snowflake, and Azure Data Lake  
• Kubernetes, Helm, Terraform, Docker, and CI/CD practices  
• SQL, T-SQL, PostgreSQL, MS SQL Server, APIs, and data integrations  
• Data Vault 2.0, DWH, Data Marts, and scalable data modeling  
• Performance optimization, cost optimization, reliability, and production support

I am strongest when teams need more than isolated data engineering tasks: architecture decisions, delivery direction, engineering standards, platform reliability, and a clear path to internal ownership.

Best fit:

• Companies building or modernizing cloud data platforms  
• Teams with fragile pipelines or unclear data ownership  
• Projects involving Lakehouse, Spark, Airflow, Kafka, Azure, AWS, or Databricks  
• Companies that need a Technical Data Lead before or while building an internal data team  
• Consulting or B2B engagements around architecture, PoC/MVP delivery, and platform improvement

Based in Wrocław, Poland. Working remotely with international teams.
