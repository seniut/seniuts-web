# seniuts.com Positioning

## Company identity

seniuts.com is a founder-led data consulting practice based in Poland and working globally.

seniuts.com helps companies design, launch, and transfer production-ready data platform foundations with a clear path to internal ownership.

The company is built around senior technical leadership, hands-on data engineering, architecture design, delivery direction, and team enablement.

## One-line positioning

seniuts.com helps companies launch cloud-native data platforms and build internal ownership without long-term vendor dependency.

## Core message

We help companies move from data chaos to a production-ready data platform foundation that their internal team can own.

Vasili Seniuts is a Technical Data Lead and Data Platform Consultant with senior hands-on experience in cloud-native data platforms, distributed data processing, orchestration, streaming, Lakehouse architecture, CI/CD, and team enablement.

The value is not only implementation, but also:

## Main offer

Architecture Audit → PoC/MVP → Launch & Transfer

## Core service model

### 1. Architecture & Strategy Audit

Purpose:
Assess the current data situation and create a practical architecture roadmap.

Typical duration:
1–2 weeks.

Outcome:
A clear target architecture, risks, quick wins, roadmap, and technical direction.

### 2. PoC / MVP Delivery

Purpose:
Build a working foundation that proves the architecture and creates delivery momentum.

Typical duration:
3–6 weeks.

Outcome:
A working data platform foundation with selected data sources, orchestration, quality checks, and documentation.

### 3. Launch & Transfer

Purpose:
Launch a production-ready foundation and transfer ownership to the client’s internal team.

Typical duration:
6–12 weeks.

Outcome:
A platform foundation, engineering standards, runbooks, onboarding, and knowledge transfer.

### 4. Optional Stabilization Support

Purpose:
Support the client for a short transition period after launch.

Outcome:
Reduced operational risk while ownership moves to the client’s team.
