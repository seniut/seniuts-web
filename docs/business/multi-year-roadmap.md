## Year 5: Scale or Exit Options

**Goal:** Sustainable business that can operate without founder's daily involvement.

### Option A: Continue Growing

- Scale to €700k–1M revenue
- Team of 8–10 people
- Vasili = Chairman/CTO (10–20% time)
- Wife = CEO (full-time)
- Hire COO or Head of Delivery

### Option B: Stay Boutique

- Maintain €400–500k revenue
- Team of 5–6 people (profitable, lifestyle business)
- Vasili = Part-time CTO
- Enjoy stability and margin

### Option C: Partial Exit

- Sell majority stake to private equity or strategic buyer
- Valuation: 2–3× revenue = €800k–1.5M
- Vasili stays as advisor (2 years earn-out)
- Wife can stay as CEO or exit

### Option D: Full Exit

- Sell 100% to strategic buyer (larger consultancy)
- Valuation: 1.5–2.5× revenue (with earn-out)
- Both founders exit after transition period

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk                           | Impact   | Mitigation                                        |
| ------------------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **No clients in Year 1**       | Critical | Parallel B2B contract, extend runway              |
| **Key client churns**          | High     | Diversify, never >30% revenue from one client     |
| **Bad hire**                   | Medium   | Start part-time, trial period, clear expectations |
| **Vasili illness/burnout**     | Critical | Wife backup, document processes, hire early       |
| **Market downturn**            | High     | Keep costs low, maintain B2B option               |
| **Wife doesn't want CEO role** | Medium   | Hire COO instead, she stays part-time             |

### Month 12: Scale or Stay Small?
