# Legal and Administrative Checklist

## Disclaimer

**This document is NOT legal, tax, or financial advice.** It is a checklist of items to consider and discuss with appropriate professionals (lawyer, accountant, tax advisor) in Poland.

---

## Business Registration (Poland)

### Option A: Sole Proprietorship (JDG — Jednoosobowa Działalność Gospodarcza)

**Pros:**

- Simple to register (CEIDG, free, online)
- Low administrative burden
- Flexible, quick to start

**Cons:**

- Personal liability (no separation from personal assets)
- May not look as professional to enterprise clients
- All income taxed as personal income

**Best for:** Starting out, Year 1–2

### Option B: Limited Liability Company (Sp. z o.o.)

**Pros:**

- Limited liability (protects personal assets)
- Looks more professional to larger clients
- Easier to add partners, sell, or raise capital

**Cons:**

- Higher setup cost (~€1,000–2,000)
- More accounting requirements
- Minimum capital requirement (PLN 5,000)

**Best for:** Year 2+ if scaling, or if working with enterprise clients

### Recommendation

- **Start:** JDG (Sole Proprietorship) — simple, cheap, fast
- **Transition:** Consider Sp. z o.o. when revenue >€100k or hiring employees

## Tax Registration

### Items to Register

- [ ] **NIP** (Tax Identification Number) — automatic with CEIDG
- [ ] **VAT registration** — required if revenue >PLN 200k/year or invoicing EU clients (discuss with accountant)
- [ ] **ZUS** (Social Insurance) — mandatory, various options (discuss with accountant)

### Tax Options in Poland

| Option                 | Description                     | Good For                   |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Progressive tax**    | 12% / 32% on income             | Lower income, high costs   |
| **Flat tax (19%)**     | 19% on profit                   | Higher income, lower costs |
| **Ryczałt (lump sum)** | % on revenue (e.g., 12% for IT) | Very low costs, simpler    |

**Consult an accountant** to determine best option based on your expected revenue, costs, and situation.

## Contract Templates

### Consulting Agreement (Master Service Agreement)

**What it covers:**

- Scope of services
- Payment terms
- Intellectual property ownership
- Confidentiality
- Liability limitations
- Termination clauses

**Get from:**

- Lawyer (Poland-based, tech/consulting experience)
- Or: Adapt template and have lawyer review

**Key clauses to include:**

| Clause                      | Why Important                              |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Scope definition**        | Prevents scope creep                       |
| **Payment terms**           | Cash flow (net 14–30)                      |
| **IP assignment**           | Client owns deliverables (usually)         |
| **Confidentiality**         | Protects both parties                      |
| **Limitation of liability** | Caps your risk (1x contract value typical) |
| **Termination**             | How to exit if needed                      |
| **Governing law**           | Polish law for Polish entity               |

### Statement of Work (SOW)

**What it covers:**

- Specific engagement scope
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Price
- Acceptance criteria

**Used:** Per-project, attached to Master Agreement

### NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement)

**What it covers:**

- What information is confidential
- How it can be used
- Duration of obligation

**Notes:**

- Most clients will send their NDA
- Have a mutual NDA template for when you share first
- Review carefully before signing (reasonable term: 2–3 years, not indefinite)

## Invoicing

### Invoice Requirements (Poland)

- [ ] Sequential invoice number
- [ ] Date of issue
- [ ] Seller details (name, address, NIP)
- [ ] Buyer details (name, address, NIP/VAT)
- [ ] Description of services
- [ ] Amount (net + VAT if applicable)
- [ ] Payment terms
- [ ] Bank account details

### Tools

| Tool              | Cost            | Notes                                     |
| ----------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **InFakt**        | PLN 30–80/month | Popular in Poland, accounting integration |
| **Fakturownia**   | PLN 20–60/month | Simple invoicing                          |
| **Wave**          | Free            | Good for basics                           |
| **Google Sheets** | Free            | Manual but works                          |

### VAT for EU Clients

- **EU B2B (with VAT number):** Reverse charge, 0% VAT, include client's VAT number
- **EU B2C (no VAT number):** Polish VAT (23%) applies
- **Non-EU (US, UK):** Export of services, 0% VAT

**Always consult accountant for VAT obligations.**

## Insurance

### Professional Indemnity Insurance (OC zawodowa)

**What it covers:** Claims from clients for errors, negligence, or failure to deliver.

**Recommended:** Yes, especially when working with larger clients.

**Typical cost:** €200–500/year for €100–500k coverage.

**Where to get:** PZU, Warta, Allianz (ask for "OC zawodowa dla IT / konsultantów")

### General Liability (OC działalności)

**What it covers:** Third-party injury or property damage.

**Recommended:** Lower priority for consulting (no physical work).

## Intellectual Property

### Default Rule

- **Employee:** Employer owns work product (usually)
- **Contractor:** Contractor owns work product unless assigned

### For Consulting Engagements

**Standard practice:** Client owns all deliverables (code, documentation, architecture).

**In contract:**

> "All work product created under this engagement shall be the exclusive property of the Client upon payment."

**What consultant retains:**

- Pre-existing IP (tools, templates, methods)
- General knowledge and skills
- Right to anonymized case studies (if agreed)

### License for Pre-existing IP

If using your own templates or code:

> "Consultant grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use Consultant's pre-existing tools and templates included in deliverables."

## Data Protection (GDPR)

### When It Applies

- Processing EU personal data
- Any client in EU

### As a Consultant

- **Data Processor:** When handling client's customer data
- **Data Controller:** For your own business (client contact info, email list)

### Checklist

- [ ] Privacy policy on website (if collecting emails)
- [ ] Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with clients when handling their data
- [ ] Secure storage of client data
- [ ] Delete client data after engagement (unless retention agreed)

### Website

If collecting email addresses (newsletter, contact form):

- [ ] Privacy policy page
- [ ] Cookie notice (if using analytics)
- [ ] Consent checkbox (optional but safer)

## Banking

### Business Bank Account

**Recommended:** Separate account for business income/expenses.

**Options in Poland:**

- **Nest Bank** — Free for JDG, good for starters
- **mBank** — Business account, reasonable fees
- **Revolut Business** — Multi-currency, good for EU/international
- **Wise Business** — Best for multi-currency, low fees

### Multi-Currency Considerations

If invoicing in EUR:

- Open EUR sub-account
- Convert when rates favorable
- Wise or Revolut often better rates than traditional banks

## Accounting

### DIY vs Accountant

| Option              | Cost              | Best For                        |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **DIY**             | Time only         | Very simple, low volume         |
| **Online service**  | PLN 100–300/month | Standard invoicing, basic taxes |
| **Full accountant** | PLN 300–800/month | Sp. z o.o., complex situations  |

### Recommendation

- **Year 1 (JDG):** Online accounting service (InFakt, ifirma)
- **Year 2+ (or Sp. z o.o.):** Full accountant

### Keep Records Of

- All invoices (issued and received)
- All expenses with receipts
- Bank statements
- Contracts
- Time tracking (if billing hourly)

- [ ] Accounting solution chosen
- [ ] Master Service Agreement template (lawyer-reviewed)

- [ ] NDA template (mutual)
- [ ] Invoice template
- [ ] Professional email (vasili@seniuts.com or similar)

### Before Working with Client

- [ ] Contract signed (MSA + SOW)
- [ ] NDA signed (if required)
- [ ] Payment terms agreed
- [ ] Invoice details collected (company name, address, VAT/NIP)
- [ ] Deposit invoiced (if applicable, 30–50%)

### After Engagement

- [ ] Final invoice sent
- [ ] Payment received
- [ ] Client feedback collected
- [ ] Case study drafted (if approved)
- [ ] Documentation handed over
- [ ] Project files archived

## Red Flags to Avoid

| Red Flag                    | Risk                | Action                                |
| --------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| No contract                 | No legal protection | Always sign before work               |
| Verbal agreement only       | Disputes            | Get in writing (email minimum)        |
| Unlimited liability         | Personal risk       | Cap liability in contract             |
| Work before payment terms   | Cash flow           | Agree terms before starting           |
| NDA with no expiration      | Burden forever      | Negotiate reasonable term (2–3 years) |
| Client owns pre-existing IP | Lose your tools     | Carve out pre-existing IP             |
| No termination clause       | Trapped             | Include exit clause                   |
