# Competitive Analysis

## Market Overview

seniuts.com operates in the data platform consulting market, specifically targeting:

- Scale-ups and mid-size companies in EU/UK/US
- Databricks/Spark cost optimization and lakehouse architecture
- Launch-and-transfer delivery model

## seniuts Competitive Advantages

### Primary Advantages

1. **Cost optimization specialization**
   - Direct proof (25% Databricks cost reduction)
   - ROI-based selling ("pay €10k, save €50k")
   - Underserved by competitors

2. **Founder-led engagement**
   - CTO-level conversations directly with delivery lead
   - No account manager, no junior staff
   - Accountability and quality control

3. **Transfer model**
   - Explicit exit strategy
   - Documentation and handover built-in
   - Attractive to clients burned by vendor lock-in

4. **Speed**
   - No sales cycle bureaucracy
   - Start within days, not months
   - Lean delivery (no large team overhead)

5. **Price/value ratio**
   - Senior expertise at boutique pricing
   - Fixed-scope = predictable cost
   - Not Accenture prices, not Upwork quality

### Secondary Advantages

6. **Modern tech stack expertise**
   - Databricks, Delta Lake, Lakehouse
   - Airflow, Dagster, Kafka
   - Not legacy DWH focus

7. **Poland-based**
   - EU timezone overlap
   - English fluency
   - Cost advantage vs Western EU/UK consultancies

8. **Multi-cloud**
   - AWS and Azure (not single-cloud lock-in)
   - Neutral advisor on cloud choice

## How to Win Against Each Competitor

### vs Polish consultancies (GetInData, etc.)

**Situation:** Client comparing seniuts to larger Polish firm.

**Message:**

> "GetInData is great for large-scale staffing. For a focused architecture engagement where you want the senior person doing the work — not managing juniors — we're a better fit. You'll work directly with me, no account manager in between."

**When to compete:** Architecture-focused work, cost optimization, smaller budgets.
**When to pass:** Large team staffing needs, enterprise procurement requirements.

---

### vs Freelancers

**Situation:** Client considering hiring freelancer from Upwork.

**Message:**

> "Freelancers are good for extending your team with hands. This is different — I'm bringing architecture expertise and a transfer methodology. You get a working platform and documentation your team can operate, not just billable hours."

**When to compete:** Architecture, PoC, knowledge transfer needed.
**When to pass:** Pure staff augmentation, client just needs extra hands.

---

### vs Big consulting

**Situation:** Client mentioned Accenture/Deloitte proposal.

**Message:**

> "For a €200k engagement with 10 consultants, they're the right choice. For a €20k architecture audit where you want hands-on technical depth and speed — we deliver in weeks what they deliver in months, at a fraction of the cost."

**When to compete:** Smaller budgets, speed needed, technical depth required.
**When to pass:** Enterprise RFP, large transformation, political cover needed.
