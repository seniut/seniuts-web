# Niche Specialization Strategy

## Why Specialize

Generic positioning ("data platform consulting") competes with:

- Thousands of freelancers on Upwork/Toptal
- Polish consultancies (GetInData, DS Stream, 10+ others)
- Big 4 / system integrators
- Internal hiring

Specialized positioning wins because:

- Easier to remember ("the Spark cost optimization guy")
- Higher perceived value (specialist > generalist)
- Better conversion (prospect thinks "this is exactly for me")
- Premium pricing (€1,000–1,200/day vs €600–800/day)
- Word of mouth ("I know someone who does exactly that")

## Recommended Primary Niche

### Option A: Databricks/Spark Lakehouse — Cost & Performance (Recommended)

**Positioning:**

> seniuts.com helps scale-ups reduce Databricks/Spark compute costs by 20–40% and build lakehouse foundations their team can operate.

**Why this niche:**

- Direct proof point (25% cost reduction at scale)
- High-value problem (Databricks bills are painful)
- Clear ROI (€50k cost reduction > €10k audit fee)
- Growing market (Databricks adoption accelerating)
- Underserved (big consultancies don't focus on cost)

**Target client:**

- Scale-up or mid-size company
- Using Databricks or Spark
- Monthly compute bill €10k–€200k
- Pain: "costs are growing faster than data volume"

**Entry offer:**

- Databricks/Spark Cost & Architecture Audit (€6,000–€10,000)
- Deliverable: cost reduction roadmap with quick wins

**Upsell path:**

- PoC implementing top 3 cost optimizations
- Full platform architecture refactor

---

### Option B: First Data Platform for Series A/B Startups

**Positioning:**

> seniuts.com helps Series A/B startups build their first production data platform in 6–12 weeks, then transfers ownership to the internal team.

**Why this niche:**

- Proof point (Lyte — startup from scratch)
- Clear buyer (CTO, Head of Engineering at funded startup)
- Time-sensitive problem (post-funding, need to scale data)
- Natural transfer point (startup hires data team after foundation)

**Target client:**

- Series A/B startup
- 30–150 employees
- Raised €5M–€30M
- Pain: "we have data but no platform"

**Entry offer:**

- Data Platform Architecture Sprint (€5,000–€8,000, 1 week)
- Deliverable: target architecture + 90-day roadmap

### Option C: Data Platform for AdTech / MarTech
