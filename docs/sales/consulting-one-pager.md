# Data Platform Consulting

**Architecture Audit → PoC/MVP → Launch & Transfer**

Help companies design, build, and improve production-ready data platforms — without creating long-term vendor dependency.

---

## Who This Is For

- Companies building or modernizing a cloud data platform
- Teams with fragile pipelines, unclear ownership, or unreliable reporting
- Software houses needing senior data platform expertise on a project basis
- Scale-ups that need a Technical Data Lead before hiring an internal team

## Typical Problems

- No clear data platform roadmap or target architecture
- Fragile ETL/ELT pipelines that slow down the business
- Slow or unreliable reporting and analytics
- No data quality controls, validation or monitoring
- Scaling issues with Spark, Airflow, Kafka, Databricks, Azure, or AWS
- No senior technical data leadership to drive standards and delivery

## Core Offer

**Architecture & Strategy Audit** _(1–2 weeks)_

**PoC / MVP Delivery** _(3–6 weeks)_

**Launch & Transfer** _(6–12 weeks)_
Production-ready platform foundation · data quality gates · observability · engineering standards · runbooks · team onboarding · ownership transfer
