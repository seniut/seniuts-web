# Partner & Fractional Platform Start List

This document is a practical starting point for the two fastest channels: **partners / subcontract** and **talent / fractional platforms**.

It is not a list of current relationships. It is a structured workflow and target template you can use to build the first 15–20 relevant conversations quickly.

---

## Why these two channels first

These channels are the best first move when you do not yet have a strong direct client network.

- **Partners / subcontract** gives you access to other companies' clients and trust.
- **Talent / fractional platforms** gives you a warm match network without a pure cold sales process.

Both can bring income faster than building direct inbound and direct prospecting alone. At the same time,
keep working content and community in the background — it will make later direct outreach much easier.

---

## 1. Partners / subcontract (software houses and cloud/DevOps consultancies)

### What this channel is

You are selling data platform expertise to companies that already serve clients but lack senior data platform depth.

The partner keeps the client and relationship. You supply the senior data architecture, platform delivery, and handover execution.

### Good company types

- **Software houses / custom product studios** — they build digital products and backends, but may not have Spark/Databricks/Kafka expertise.
- **Cloud / DevOps consultancies** — they manage AWS/Azure/Kubernetes/Terraform, but do not own data platform engineering.
- **BI / analytics consultancies** — they build dashboards and analytics, but the data foundation is not their core.
- **Overflow SI partners** — data practices that take overflow work from larger integration projects.

### Example of the type

Use these categories as your target archetypes, not as current partners.

- A Polish custom software house with enterprise clients and no visible data platform case studies.
- A cloud consultancy that advertises Kubernetes, Terraform, and platform engineering.
- A BI consultancy that sells Power BI/Tableau projects and needs a stronger backend.

### What to find first

| Step | What to look for                                                        |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Company with EU/UK/US clients and custom software or cloud practice     |
| 2    | Leadership titles: CTO, Founder, Head of Engineering, Delivery Director |
| 3    | Evidence of cloud infrastructure work or analytics claims               |
| 4    | Few or no visible data platform case studies                            |

### Who to contact

- CTO / Founder
- Head of Engineering
- Delivery Director
- Partnerships Manager
- Data Practice Lead (if exists)

### Simple target list skeleton

| Company                | Type              | Contact          | Notes                                 | First outreach status |
| ---------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Example Software House | Software house    | CTO              | builds custom SaaS for finance        | Not contacted         |
| Example Cloud Partner  | Cloud consultancy | Founder          | AWS / Terraform, no data case studies | Not contacted         |
| Example BI Shop        | BI consultancy    | Head of Delivery | does dashboards, needs data backend   | Not contacted         |

### First message angle

Use the partner format from [../business/outreach-playbook.md](../business/outreach-playbook.md).

Example:

> Hi [Name],
>
> I work with software houses and cloud consultancies that sometimes need deeper data platform expertise for client projects — Spark/Databricks, Airflow, Lakehouse architecture, and reliable platform handover.
>
> I can be a partner on those projects: you keep the client relationship, I handle the data layer and delivery.
>
> Would you be open to a short call to see if there is a fit?
>
> Best,\
> Vasili Seniuts
> seniuts.com

### Why this is useful now

This channel gives you the fastest way to work with clients while you still build direct proof.

If a partner wants a data architecture review, you can deliver an Architecture Audit as a partner-backed engagement,
which is a strong step toward your direct consulting brand.

## 2. Talent / fractional platforms

### What this channel is

Curated platforms and marketplaces are where clients already go to find senior technical talent.

This is not the same as cold outreach. A successful application means the platform will present you to clients who need your skills.

### Good platform categories

- **Senior delivery marketplaces** — Toptal, Gun.io, Braintrust, A.Team, Lemon.io
- **Fractional/interim leadership platforms** — Go Fractional, Continuum, Catalant, Graphite, Expert360
- **Expert/advisory networks** — GLG, Dialectica, Guidepoint (good for advisory-style short engagements)

### What to apply for first

- Fractional Technical Data Lead / Data Platform Architect / Senior Data Engineer roles
- Contract roles with cloud data platform, Spark and orchestration focus
- Interim leader roles that explicitly mention part-time or advisory delivery

### Simple platform tracking sheet

| Platform      | Role type                     | Status  | Notes                             |
| ------------- | ----------------------------- | ------- | --------------------------------- |
| Toptal        | Senior contract data engineer | Applied | profile set up, pending interview |
| Go Fractional | Fractional Data Lead          | Applied | waiting feedback                  |
| Braintrust    | Senior Data Platform          | Applied | profile complete                  |

### Profile focus

For platforms, your profile should highlight:

- senior data platform architecture
- hands-on Spark/Databricks cost optimization
- Lakehouse delivery on AWS + Azure
- Architecture Audit / PoC delivery / transfer model
- leadership and team enablement

Use the same key proof points from your website, but framed for a platform profile:

- 8+ years of data engineering
- Spark/Databricks cost reductions on high-volume platform
- Azure + AWS lakehouse and orchestration experience
- delivery with clear handover and operational runbooks

### Why this is useful now

Platform work is your best source of matched opportunities when you cannot rely on warm introductions.

Each successful platform project is also a future direct proof point — a short testimonial, a case note, and a realistic way to grow your network.

## Practical next step

1. Create a single spreadsheet with two tabs: **Partners** and **Platforms**.
2. Add 15–20 partner targets and 5–10 platform applications.
3. Use the partner message and the platform profile guidance above.
4. Track every outreach step and update the list weekly.

## Quick partner / platform starter checklist

- [ ] Pick 15 partner companies from software houses, cloud consultancies, and BI shops
- [ ] Find 1–2 decision makers for each company
- [ ] Write and send the first partner message to 3–5 targets
- [ ] Apply to 3–4 platforms as a senior/technical data lead
- [ ] Write or refine one platform profile using your proof points
- [ ] Review progress weekly and expand the list based on replies

---

## Notes

- Do not use this document as a cold sales pitch list to random companies.
- Focus on relevance: fewer high-quality partner targets is better than many generic ones.
- Keep content and community work running in the background; it makes this direct activity more effective over time.
