# Target Clients — Vasili Seniuts

## Goal

Build a structured list of potential clients and partners for Technical Data Lead / Data Platform Consulting engagements.

The first goal is not immediate sales.

The first goal is to create relevant conversations with people and companies that may need:

- data platform architecture
- cloud data platform modernization
- Lakehouse / Spark / Airflow / Kafka expertise
- PoC/MVP data platform delivery
- technical data leadership
- platform reliability improvement
- team enablement and internal ownership

Main strengths:

- data platform architecture

# 1. Target segments

## Segment A — Software houses

### Why this segment matters

Software houses often have client relationships but may not have strong senior data platform expertise internally.

They may need help when their client asks for:

- data platform architecture
- Lakehouse implementation
- Airflow / Spark / Kafka work
- Azure / AWS data pipelines
- analytics platform foundations
- data engineering technical leadership

### Offer angle

External Technical Data Lead / Data Platform Consultant for client projects.

Message angle:

> You keep the client relationship. I help cover the senior data architecture / platform delivery part when needed.

### Priority

High.

### Target count

20 companies.

### Who to contact

- CEO / Founder
- CTO
- Head of Engineering
- Delivery Director
- Partnerships Manager
- Data Practice Lead, if exists

### Good signals

- They build custom software for clients
- They mention cloud, backend, AI, analytics, or enterprise systems
- They do not have a strong visible data engineering practice
- They work with EU / UK / US clients
- They have case studies around software platforms but not many around data platforms

---

## Segment B — Cloud / DevOps consultancies

### Why this segment matters

Cloud and DevOps consultancies often work with infrastructure, Kubernetes, Terraform, and cloud platforms, but may need deeper data platform expertise.

### Offer angle

Data platform architecture layer for cloud clients.

Relevant topics:

- Airflow on Kubernetes
- Databricks / Synapse / Snowflake
- Lakehouse architecture
- Spark workloads
- Kafka-based pipelines
- Terraform / CI/CD for data workflows
- production data platform operations

### Priority

High.

### Target count

10 companies.

### Who to contact

- CTO
- Cloud Practice Lead
- DevOps Lead
- Founder
- Delivery Manager

### Good signals

- They mention Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, Azure
- They work with cloud migrations
- They support platform engineering
- They do not clearly offer senior data platform architecture
- They may have clients asking about analytics / AI / data foundations

---

## Segment C — Scale-ups

### Why this segment matters

Scale-ups often grow faster than their data platform maturity.

Typical problems:

- fragile pipelines
- too much logic in scripts
- unclear ownership
- slow reporting
- unreliable data
- no clear Lakehouse / warehouse architecture
- not enough senior data leadership

### Offer angle

Architecture & Strategy Audit as the first step.

Message angle:

> I help teams understand their current data platform state, risks, and practical roadmap before investing in a larger build.

### Priority

Medium to high.

### Target count

10 companies.

### Who to contact

- CTO
- VP Engineering
- Head of Data
- Data Lead
- Analytics Lead
- COO, if the company is smaller

### Good signals

- recently funded
- growing engineering team
- hiring data engineers / analytics engineers
- multiple data-related job posts
- public mentions of AI / analytics / reporting / platform work
- product generates operational/event/customer data

---

## Segment D — Data / AI startups

### Why this segment matters

AI and analytics startups often need reliable data foundations before their AI/ML or analytics products can scale.

### Offer angle

PoC/MVP data platform foundation or architecture review.

Relevant topics:

- data ingestion
- data quality
- Lakehouse / warehouse structure
- batch and streaming pipelines
- model-ready data
- platform reliability

### Priority

Medium.

### Target count

5 companies.

### Who to contact

- Founder
- CTO
- Head of Engineering
- Head of Data / ML

### Good signals

- AI/ML product depends on data quality
- data pipelines are core to the product
- company is early but growing
- team does not yet have a mature data platform function

---

## Segment E — Warm network

### Why this segment matters

Warm network is the fastest path to trust.

These people may not become clients directly, but they can:

- refer you
- introduce you to CTOs
- connect you with software houses
- recommend you for B2B consulting
- tell you about hidden opportunities

### Offer angle

Soft update, not direct sale.

Message angle:

> I’m now focusing more on Technical Data Lead / Data Platform Consulting work. If you hear about teams struggling with data platform direction, fragile pipelines, or unclear ownership, I’d be happy to help.

### Priority

Very high.

### Target count

10–20 people.

### Who to contact

- former colleagues
- former managers
- tech leads
- architects
- recruiters who know you
- founders / CTOs in your network
- people from previous projects

# 2. Lead qualification checklist

Before adding a company to the target list, check at least 2–3 of these signals:

- They work with data-heavy products or platforms
- They mention analytics, AI, reporting, events, finance, ads, marketplace, logistics, SaaS, or operations
- They are hiring data engineers, analytics engineers, data platform engineers, or cloud engineers
- They use AWS, Azure, Databricks, Snowflake, Kafka, Spark, Airflow, or similar tools
- They have grown recently or received funding
- They have a software product with operational data
- They are a software house that may need data expertise for clients
- They have public signs of platform modernization
- They have no obvious senior data platform leadership visible
- They are in Europe, UK, US, or remote-friendly markets

They have growing data needs but no clear platform architecture or internal ownership.

Possible offer:

Architecture & Strategy Audit.

## For data / AI startups

Possible problem:

They need reliable, model-ready or analytics-ready data foundations.

Possible offer:

PoC/MVP data foundation or platform architecture review.

## For warm network

Possible problem:

They may know someone who needs data architecture, platform improvement, or technical leadership.

Possible offer:

Intro conversation or referral.

# 7. Weekly workflow

## Weekly target

Minimum:

- add 10 new leads
- research 10 leads
- send 10 first messages
- send 5 follow-ups
- book 1 intro call

Strong week:

- add 20 new leads
- send 20 first messages
- send 10 follow-ups
- book 2–3 intro calls

## Weekly review questions

At the end of each week, review:

- Which segment replied most?
- Which message worked best?
- Which offer angle got interest?
- Which roles responded: CTO, Founder, Head of Data, Delivery Manager?
- Were responses warm, neutral, or negative?
- Did anyone mention budget / timing / need?
- Should the positioning be adjusted?

# 11. First offer

Do not start by selling a large platform build.

Start with:

## Architecture & Strategy Audit

Purpose:

Understand the current data platform state and identify practical next steps.

Possible audit scope:

- current architecture review
- pipeline reliability review
- orchestration review
- Lakehouse / DWH structure review
- cost and performance risks
- data ownership and maintainability risks
- roadmap for PoC/MVP or platform improvement

Possible output:

- short written assessment
- architecture diagram
- risks and quick wins
- recommended roadmap
- optional PoC/MVP scope

This is easier to sell than a large project and can lead to PoC/MVP delivery.

---

# 12. Important principles

Do not try to look like a large company.

Position honestly as:

- senior independent consultant
- hands-on Technical Data Lead
- boutique consulting partner
- external data platform lead
- B2B consulting partner

Avoid saying:

- we are a large team
- we can do everything
- we have many clients
- we provide unlimited support
- we own the project forever

Strong honest positioning:

> I can help you understand the architecture, build momentum, and set up the foundation so your team can take ownership.
