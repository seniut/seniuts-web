# seniuts.com — Website Content Requirements

## Global requirements

The website must communicate quickly and clearly:

1. What seniuts.com does.
2. Who it helps.
3. What problems it solves.
4. How the engagement works.
5. Why the handover model is different.
6. How to start.

All public website copy must be in English.

## Recommended pages

### Home

Purpose:
Give a fast executive-level overview.

Required sections:

1. Hero
   - Clear headline
   - Clear subheadline
   - Primary CTA: Book an Architecture Audit
   - Secondary CTA: View Services

2. Problem section
   - Data chaos
   - Fragile pipelines
   - No clear ownership
   - Slow reporting
   - No scalable foundation

3. Service preview
   - Architecture & Strategy Audit
   - PoC / MVP Delivery
   - Launch & Transfer

4. How we work
   - Discovery
   - Architecture
   - Delivery
   - Stabilization
   - Transfer

5. Why seniuts.com
   - Senior expertise
   - Practical architecture
   - Product mindset
   - Handover-first model

6. Final CTA
   - Start with an Architecture Audit

### Services

Purpose:
Explain the three core packages.

Required packages:

1. Architecture & Strategy Audit
2. PoC / MVP Delivery
3. Launch & Transfer

Each package should include:

- who it is for
- expected duration
- deliverables
- outcome

### About

Purpose:
Build trust.

Required content:

- Founder background
- Why seniuts.com exists
- Data engineering and architecture expertise
- Practical, hands-on delivery mindset
- Global consulting from Poland / EU

Avoid making it look like a personal CV.

### Contact

Purpose:
Make it easy to start a conversation.

Required content:

- Short intro
- Email
- LinkedIn
- Contact form if available
- CTA: Book an Architecture Audit

## Messaging rules

Prefer short sentences.

Avoid vague claims.

Every section should answer one of these questions:

- What problem do we solve?
- What result does the client get?
- Why should the client trust us?
- What is the next step?
