## Engagement style

I usually work best as:

- Hands-on Technical Data Lead
- Data Platform Consultant
- Data Platform Architect
- PoC/MVP delivery lead
- Lakehouse and orchestration consultant
- Team enablement and handover partner

My preferred engagement model is practical and outcome-driven:

1. Understand the current situation, constraints, and business goals
2. Define target architecture and delivery roadmap
3. Build or improve the platform foundation
4. Establish engineering standards, reliability patterns, and documentation
5. Help the internal team take ownership

The goal is not to create long-term dependency. The goal is to help teams build a maintainable data platform foundation they can operate and evolve.
